🏁 Apex is a free Formula 1 live wallpaper for Windows 10 and 11. It sits on your desktop behind your icons and keeps you up to date with the championship while you use your PC.

What it shows

- 📅 The next Grand Prix: every session of the weekend (practice, qualifying, sprint and race) in your local time, with a countdown.
- 🏆 Driver and constructor standings, updated automatically after every race.
- 🗺️ The circuit map of the next GP.
- 🌦️ Weather at the circuit, with the track’s local time and the weekend forecast.
- ⏱️ Clock and date, in 6 different styles.
- 📈 A telemetry button (F1 Tempo) you can click right from the desktop.

The background can be an animated scene with a race car, whose sky follows the time of day, or your own photos, which change by themselves.

🎨 Fully customizable from the Apex window (just double-click the tray icon): what to show and where, the size of every widget, transparency, font, line color (even automatic, based on your photo), clock style, language and monitor.

⚡ Light and unobtrusive: it pauses while you play games or watch full-screen videos, has a power-saving mode for laptops, and updates itself.

🌍 Available in English, Italian, Spanish, French and German. Each PC just needs a free activation by email.
